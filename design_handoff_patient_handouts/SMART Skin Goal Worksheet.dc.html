My SMART Skin Goal

Small, specific goals work better than big ones. Fill this out for one thing you want to change.

Specific
What exactly will you do?
e.g. “My goal is to moisturize my elbows twice a day”
Measurable
How will you know you did it?
e.g. “I will track this on my phone”
Achievable
What will make this easy to keep up?
e.g. “I will set two reminder alarms”
Relevant
Why does this matter to you?
e.g. “This will heal my skin and reduce itch”
Time-bound
When will you start, and for how long?
e.g. “I will do this daily for two weeks, then reassess”
