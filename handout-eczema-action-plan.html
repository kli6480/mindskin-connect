My Eczema Action Plan

Know what to do at every stage of a flare, before it gets worse. Fill this in with your care team and keep it somewhere handy.

Green zone — skin is calm
Your everyday routine
Amber zone — skin is starting to flare
Itching, redness, or dry patches appearing
Red zone — flare isn't improving or looks infected
Spreading fast, oozing/crusting, fever, or no relief

MindSkin Connect · University of Alberta Dermatology — this plan does not replace medical advice. Always follow your clinician's instructions.

MindSkin Connect is for general education and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment — always talk to your doctor or dermatologist about your own condition.
