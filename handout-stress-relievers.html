Quick Stress Relievers

Short bursts of calm still count. Try these anywhere.

1

Mindful Breathing

1–5 minutes

How

Focus on your breath. Notice the air entering and leaving your body. If your mind wanders, gently bring it back.

When

During a work or study break, waiting in line, before a stressful meeting, or when you feel itch escalating.

3:00
2

Quick Body Scan

2–5 minutes

How

Mentally scan your body from toes to head, noticing any tension without judgment. Consciously try to release it.

When

While commuting (if you're not driving), during a lunch break, before sleep.

2:00
3

Sensory Grounding — 5-4-3-2-1

1–3 minutes

How

Engage your senses and name 5 things you can see, 4 things you can touch, 3 things you can hear, 2 things you can smell, and 1 thing you can taste (or one positive thought).

When

If you're feeling overwhelmed or anxious, to bring yourself back to the present moment.

1:00

Pick one. Try it three times this week.
