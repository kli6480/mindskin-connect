Myth vs. Fact

Myth

[Placeholder myth: "HS is caused by poor hygiene. If I just cleaned the affected areas better, it would go away."]

Fact

[Placeholder fact: "HS is a chronic inflammatory condition linked to immune dysregulation and genetics — not hygiene habits."]

Myth

[Placeholder myth: "Stress has nothing to do with how bad my HS gets. My skin flares are random."]

Fact

[Placeholder fact: "Research shows psychological stress can trigger or worsen HS flares through neuroinflammatory pathways."]

Myth

[Placeholder myth: "The emotional side of HS is something I just have to push through on my own."]

Fact

[Placeholder fact: "Mind-body approaches and working with your care team on the emotional impact can meaningfully improve quality of life with HS."]

Key points

Read the key points Hide the key points
  • [Placeholder: HS is a chronic inflammatory condition — not a result of poor hygiene or personal choices.]
  • [Placeholder: Stress activates the immune system in ways that can worsen HS inflammation and trigger flares.]
  • [Placeholder: Emotional distress is a recognised part of living with HS, and your care team can help.]
  • [Placeholder: Telling your care team how HS affects you emotionally helps them provide more complete care.]
  • [Placeholder: There are effective mind-body strategies that can reduce the impact of stress on your skin.]

Bring this to your appointment

  • [Placeholder: Have you noticed a link between stress or emotions and your HS flares?]
  • [Placeholder: What emotional impact has HS had on your daily life, relationships, or work?]
  • [Placeholder: Are there mind-body supports — such as counselling or peer support — you would like to explore?]
  • [Placeholder: Is there anything about your HS that you find hard to bring up with your care team?]
