About this resource

A free, evidence-based program

MindSkin Connect delivers the Mind–Skin Health for Atopic Dermatitis (MSHAD) modules, developed by the University of Alberta Division of Dermatology. The content is grounded in the published literature and informed by scoping reviews of psychodermatology and behavioural approaches to chronic skin disease — and every module cites its sources.

Built to support the whole patient

These free resources support the psychodermatologic management of chronic skin conditions — addressing the emotional, behavioural, and lifestyle dimensions of eczema alongside medical care, never in place of it. The platform collects no personal information and is licensed under Creative Commons (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0).

Bring it to your clinic

Clinicians interested in offering MindSkin Connect to their patients — for example, through a waiting-room QR code — are warmly invited to reach out. We can share the modules, print-ready materials, and guidance for folding them into patient education.

Contact the program team

MindSkin Connect is for general education and does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment — always talk to your doctor or dermatologist about your own condition. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, call 911, or the AHS Mental Health Helpline at 1-877-303-2642.
