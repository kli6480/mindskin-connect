Progressive Muscle Relaxation (PMR)

Tense, then release — one muscle group at a time.

Progressive muscle relaxation is a technique where you systematically tense and then relax different muscle groups in your body. It helps you become more aware of physical tension and learn to release it. This can reduce overall stress and may ease muscle tension.

How to do it

  1. 1

    Find a comfortable position, sitting or lying down.

  2. 2

    Start with your feet. Tense the muscles for 5–10 seconds, then release completely for 20–30 seconds, noticing the difference.

  3. 3

    Move up your body — calves, thighs, buttocks, abdomen, chest, arms, hands, shoulders, neck, face — tensing and relaxing each group.

Guided session

About 6 minutes. Tense for 5 seconds, release for 10. You can stop any time.

Tips

  • Tense firmly, but never to the point of pain.
  • Breathe out as you release.
  • 10–15 minutes is plenty. You don't have to do the whole body every time.
  • If a muscle group is sore or injured, skip it.
